It is a truth universally acknowledged that a single man in possession of a good fortune must be in want of a wife.

Elizabeth was a young woman who lived with her family.

Her mother wanted her daughters to marry wealthy men.

Mr. Darcy was a rich man, but he seemed proud and unfriendly.

Elizabeth did not like the way he treated other people.

The more she learned about him, the more she began to question her first impression.

She believed that people should not judge others too quickly.
